## Aphid infestation

At the leaf stage BBCH 65 immediately at the onset of flowering, plants will be infested with one adult G1 aphids coming from synchronised populations. 

Individual faba plants in cages 2, 4, 6, 8 will be infested with:  
  - One _A. pisum_ P123 adults from the G1 generation (cages 2 and 4) or,  
  - One _A. fabae_ AF001 adults from the G1 generation (cages 6 and 8).

## Germination tests

At 20°C in the dark during 120h (5 days) in 15ml (or 25ml for Canakkale) tap water on two layers of filter paper.  

For each genotype, 30 seeds were placed in 2 (3 for Canakkale) Petri dishes of 88mm diameter with two layers of germination filter paper (85mm diameter, ref 074141, Dominique Dutscher).
