## Goal

To compare the fitness (number and weight of seeds) of genotypes shown to be contrasted for their resistance to aphids in controlled conditions.  
Investigation of potential trade-offs between resistance and tolerance.

## Aphid infestation

At the leaf stage LP.XX immediately before flowering, plants will be infested with adult aphids coming from synchronised populations. 

Individual faba plants in cages 2, 4, 6, 8 will be infested with:  
  - Three _A. pisum_ P123 adults from the G2 generation (cages 2 and 4) or,  
  - Three _A. fabae_ AF001 adults from the G2 generation (cages 6 and 8). 

## Metrics to collect

- Number of seeds: n_seeds  
- Hundred seed weight (HSW): hsw
- NIRS profiles of seeds.
