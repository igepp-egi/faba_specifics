# P2-XP012 Microscopic quantification of callose deposition in response to aphids

## Objectives

Quantify the amount of callose deposited in presence of aphids (A. pisum P123 and A. fabae AF001) at different time points (3, 24, 72 and 120hpi) in different _Vicia faba_ genotypes. 
If possible, also stain gel saliva around the stylet. 

## Protocol 

Hey Marc. Here is my protocol.
Cut off the leaves with the adult, still feeding aphids. Place the cut leaves in a petri dish. Place the petri dish on our CO2 table and cover it with a lid. Incubate everything with CO2 for approx. 20 seconds.
Pour tert. Butanol/PFA solution/fixative (Σ 50ml - 15ml PFA + 35ml tert.butanol) into this petri dish - be careful not to wash the aphids off the leaf.
Incubate everything for a few minutes.
Cut the leaf around the preferred aphid and always stay in the fixative.
Then remove the fixative with a pipette and pour some agarose (1%) on the separated aphids.
The next steps, such as embedding, can follow.
