# P2-XP013 Generation of a F2 population between Prevosky, 29H, Skala and sensitive genotypes. 

## Objectives

Study the segregation of the partial resistance to _A. pisum_ P123 and _A. fabae_ AF001 by making crosses between faba bean cv Prevosky (IVIP100123, geno. #7; PR/PR), 29H (IVIP102516, geno #222; PR/S), Skalla (IVIP101240, geno #106; S/PR) and two other cultivars that are sensitive to both aphids (big screen results). 

Two other genotypes (IVIP102516 and IVIP101240) were also added since they are respectively PR/S and S/PR to _A. fabae_ AF001 and _A. pisum_ P123 respectively.  

## Genotypes to confirm for aphid resistance

| IVIP  		    |  Genotype | Name          | Phenotype (screen 022-2024)                                           |
|---------------|-----------|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| IVIP100123  	| 7  	      | PREROVSKY     | Partially resistant to both aphids (PR/PR)                            |
| IVIP101736	  | 153  	    | TIANMENDOU    | Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                                        |
| IVIP100872	  | 77  	    | VERDE DE LIMA | Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                                        |
| IVIP102516    | 222       | 29H           | Resistant to _A. fabae_ and sensitive to _A. pisum_ (PR/S)            |
| IVIP101240    | 106       | SKALLA        | Sensitive to _A. fabae_ and resistant to _A. pisum_ (S/PR)            |

These genotypes should be confirmed using a fecundity test with one L1-G2 larvae for their sensitivity to _A. fabae_ and _A. pisum_. 

Prevosky can be used as the internal control as this genotype has been extensively confirmed (see table below).

As a sensitive control:
- For _A. fabae_ AF001, IVIP100355 (KHASH) can be used (confirmed independently; N=41).  
- For _A. pisum_ P123, IVIP103069 (ANATOLIEN) can be used (confirmed independently; N=56).
