# P2-XP013 Generation of a F2 population between Prevosky and sensitive genotypes. 

## Objectives

Study the segregation of the partial resistance to _A. pisum_ P123 and _A. fabae_ AF001 by making crosses between faba bean cv Prevosky (IVIP100123, geno. #7) and four other cultivars that are sensitive to both aphids (big screen results). 

## Genotypes 

| IVIP  		|  Geno# | Batch   | Phenotype (screen 2022-2024)                     |  
|---------------|--------|---------|--------------------------------------------------|
| IVIP100123  	| 7  	 | JF2094  |  Partially resistant to both aphids (PR/PR)      |
| IVIP100872	| 77  	 | JF2142  |  Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                  |
| IVIP100874	| 78  	 | JF2143  |  Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                  |
| IVIP101320	| 113  	 | JF2172  |  Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                  |
| IVIP101736	| 153  	 | JF2201  |  Sensitive to both aphids (S/S)                  |


## To be done

1) Seed multiplication of all parental lines.    
2) Crosses between IVIP100123 (PR/PR) and all other IVIPs (S/S) to get ~30 seeds per F1.   
3) Phenotype the parental lines to confirm their phenotype to _A. fabae_ or _A. pisum_ when needed:   
	- Genotype #113 is confirmed for its sensitivity to _A. pisum_ (N=55) and _A. fabae_ (N=42).  
	- Genotypes #77, #78, #113 and #153 (S/S phenotypes) have to be confirmed for their sensitivity to _A. fabae_ (3 independent XP needed).   
	- Genotypes #77, #78 and #153 have to be confirmed for their sensitivity to _A. pisum_ (one XP needed).
