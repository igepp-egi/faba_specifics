# P2-XP013 Generation of a F2 population between Prevosky and sensitive genotypes. 

## Objectives

Study the segregation of the partial resistance to _A. pisum_ P123 and _A. fabae_ AF001 by making crosses between faba bean cv Prevosky (IVIP100123, geno. #7) and two other cultivars that are sensitive to both aphids (big screen results). 

Two other genotypes (IVIP102516 and IVIP101240) were also added since they are respectively PR/S and S/PR to _A. fabae_ AF001 and _A. pisum_ P123 respectively.
