
# P2-XP018 - High aphid densities (*Aphis fabae* AF001 and *Acyrthosiphon pisum* P123amp) on *Vicia faba*

## Objectives

The first version of AphiDetect, developed during Océane Gourdin's Master 2 internship, still requires some improvements. The previously acquired data contains photos of the aphids *Acyrthosiphon pisum* and *Aphis fabae* at low and medium densities (from 0 to approximately 90 aphids per plant).

Experiment P2-XP018 allows us to verify the performance of AphiDetect at high density (100 to 250 individuals per plant). If performance were to decrease drastically, the model would be retrained with the new dataset from P2-XP018.


## Biological material

Plant :
- *Vicia faba*, IVIP101748, genotype Castel

Aphids :
- *Acyrthosiphon pisum*, P123amp, lineage collected in Mauzé-sur-le-Mignon in April 1999
- *Aphis fabae*, AF001, lineage collected at Le Rheu (Domaine de la Motte) in Spring 2023

## Protocol

**Day 1 (27/10/2025)** :

Sowing *Vicia faba* in 7x7x8 cm pots. Place in tray and in growth chamber at 20°C, 16D:8N photoperiod, 80 % relative humidity. Water once a week.

**Day 9 (04/11/2025)** : 

Adults settling on plants. For each aphid, 3 modalities :
- Installation of 1 adult on 10 plants
- Installation for 2 adults on 10 plants
- Installation for 3 adults on 10 plants

Therefore, there are 30 plants per aphid and 10 plants per density modality. That makes a total of (10*3)*2 = 60 plants

The plants are individually covered with a bag secured by an elastic band to the pot to prevent the dispersal of aphids or contamination between pots.

The bagged pots are placed on two trays (1 tray *Acyrthosiphon pisum* and 1 tray *Aphis fabae*) then placed in another climate chamber with the same conditions as before.

**Day 18 (13/11/2025)** :

Three-dimensional photography in the phenotyping booth (36 photos per plant, one every 10° to cover 360°).

Taking 2D overhead photos in counting trays (1 photo per plant).
