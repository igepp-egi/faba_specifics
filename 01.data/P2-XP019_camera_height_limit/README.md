
# A1-XP001 Camera height limit 


## Objectives

Define the maximum plant stage/plant size to maintain optimal image quality for aphid detection with a single camera.


## Biological material

Aphid :
- *Acyrthosiphon pisum*, P123amp, lineage collected in Mauzé-sur-le-Mignon in April 1999

Plants :
- *Vicia faba*, IVIP101748, genotype Castel



## Protocol

Below, all dates are written in the following format: day/month/year

Illustrations of this experiment are available in the folder : [Pictures](./Pictures-inoculation)
 

**Day 1 to Day 9 (05/01/2026 to 13/01/2026)** :

Sowing *Vicia faba* in 7x7x8 cm pots (1 seed per pot). Place in tray and in growth chamber at 20°C, 16D:8N photoperiod, 80 % relative humidity. Water once a week.

In our case, three seeds were planted on :
- 05/01/2026
- 08/01/2026
- 09/01/2026
- 12/01/2026
- 13/01/2026

**Day 15 (19/01/2026)** : 

Each plant is inoculated with two adult P123amp aphids. 

A bag is then placed around the plant and secured to the base of the pot with a rubber band. This prevents the aphids from dispersing and thus avoids contamination from one plant to another or with other experiments conducted in the same climate chamber. The tray is then returned to the climate chamber with the same conditions.


**Day X (XX/0X/2026)** :

Using the photo booth to photograph each plant in 360° (1 photo every 20°). The image quality is then analyzed locally on a computer.
