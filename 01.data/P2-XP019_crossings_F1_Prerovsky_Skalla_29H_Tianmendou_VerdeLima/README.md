# P2-XP019 Crossings to generate an F1 between Prevosky, 29H, Skala and Tianmendou or Verde de Lima genotypes. 

## Objectives

Obtain two F1 between partially resistant and sensitive faba bean lines: crosses between faba bean cv Prevosky (IVIP100123, geno. #7; PR/PR), 29H (IVIP102516, geno #222; PR/S), Skalla (IVIP101240, geno #106; S/PR) and two other cultivars that are sensitive to both aphids (big screen results). 

- Crossing number 1 : 29H with SKALLA
- Crossing number 2 : PREROVSKY with TIANMENDOU or VERDE DE LIMA

- Cross #1: between IVIP100123 (PR/PR) and either IVIP101736 (S/S) or IVIP100872 (S/S),   
- Cross #2: between Skalla (PR/S) and 29H (R/S).   

In 2026-2027, study the segregation in the F1 of the partial resistance to _A. pisum_ P123 and _A. fabae_ AF001 on the selfed F1s. 

Two other genotypes (IVIP102516 and IVIP101240) were also added since they are respectively PR/S and S/PR to _A. fabae_ AF001 and _A. pisum_ P123 respectively.

## Number of plants 

For each crossing, 10 plants of parent P1 and 10 plants of parent P2. 
Seeds will be collected in June-July.
