# P2-XP019 Crossings to generate an F1 between Prevosky, 29H, Skala and Tianmendou or Verde de Lima genotypes. 

## Objectives and summary

Determine the genetic determinism of resistance/sensitivity in F1s coming from parental contrasted Vicia faba genotypes for their resistance to _A. fabae_ and _A. pisum_.   

Genotypes are either:    
- partially resistant to both aphids (PR/PR), 
- partially resistant to _A. fabae_  (PR/S)
- partially resistant to _A. pisum_  (S/PR)
- sensitive to both aphids (S/S)

4 crossings are made using insect-proof and pollen-proof cages and bumble bees (Bombus terrestris; Koppert).  

Parents will be genotyped using genotyping arrays (AGRODIV/BELIS chips).

## Harvest and genotyping

For genotyping, one leaf piece per parental plant to be genotyped using either PCR markers or the BELIS/AGRODIV genotyping chip. 

One color code per plant to identify female parents: the idea is to harvest all female parents from the same genotype in bags in case there are parental effets (reciprocal cross from F1).
Two F1 per cross: F1-femaleP1 and F1-femaleP2.
