# Structure based on phenology

Phenotypic data were acquired in Dijon (UMR Agroécologie) on the PeaMUST Vicia faba collection (N=248 genotypes).

## Traits investigated

**Flowering start date**: calendar date.   
**SumTem (Semis-DFLO)**: ?  

**Flower color:**  
  - Banner ("étendard") color: 0 (NA) to 9 or mixed (X).  
  - Stripe color intensity on the banner: from 0 (absence) to 9 (intense).  
  - Wing petal color intensity: 1 (white), 2 (uniform color) or 3 (spotted).  
  - Spot color: 0 (absence), 1 (white), 2 (violet), 3 (dark brown), 4 (pale brown), 5 (pink), 6 (red), 7 (yellow), 8 (other color) or mixed (X).  
 
**Growth**
  - Growth type: i (indeterminate), i/2 (semi-determinate), ti (terminal inflorescence).   
  - Height at flowering onset: in centimeters.   

**Seed traits**  
- Seed color: 0 (absence), 1 (black), 2 (brown), 3 (beige), 4 (green), 5 (red), 6 (purple), 7 (yellow), 8 (cream), 9 (grey), 10 (pink) or mixed (X).  
- Seed ornementation spot: 0 (absence), 1 (concentric), 2 (stained), 3 (marbled), 5 (speckled), 6 (spotted), 7 (mottled).
