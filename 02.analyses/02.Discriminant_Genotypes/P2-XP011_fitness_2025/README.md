- Number of pods and seeds per genotype and per aphid treatment.  
- Fresh seed weight per genotype and per aphid treatment.
- Germination final rate per genotype and per aphid treatment.
- FT-NIR measures on seeds from cages outside (-/+ aphid) and from the NS1 greenhouses (- aphid) for each genotype.
