# RNAseq and metabolomics protocol

Protocol to get total RNA from aerial parts of seedlings infested or not with aphids. 

## Material  

- Liquid nitrogen   
- [Ladle ("louche")](https://fr.vwr.com/store/product/7596968/louches-manche-rond)    
- Steel funnel (ext. diameter > 300 mm; internal diameter 20 mm).   
- Lab scissors  
- Bottle brush ("goupillon")   
- Pestle (10 cm diameter)   
- Falcon 50ml RNase-free  **(?)**  
- Permanent labgrade markers    
- Stainless steel beads (3 mm diameter)  
- Cryobox cardboard [(Sarstedt 4 x 4 for 16 Falcon tubes)](https://www.sarstedt.com/fr/produits/laboratoire/portoirs-boites-de-stockage/boites-de-stockage/produit/95.64.916/)  

## Equipment   

- Paint shaker  
- Freeze-dryer [Labconco LB7948030](https://www.serlabo.fr/stoppering-tray-dryer-labconco.html)   

## Plant material

_Vicia faba_ plants of two weeks [2 to 3-leaf stage (LP.02 to LP.03)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/ontologies/po/classes/http%253A%252F%252Fpurl.obolibrary.org%252Fobo%252FPO_0007098). 

## Method

### Lyophylizer (at least 2h before)

- Turn on the vacuum pump, cold trap and the cooling equipment. 

### Falcon tube and beads weight (before)

- Number Falcon tubes (1 to N)
- Place three stainless steel beads in each 50ml falcon tube.  
- Weight tube + beads (tare).
- Write Falcon + beads total weight. 

### Sample collection

1. After 2 weeks of growth, above-ground parts of the plant seedlings are harvested above first leaf node.    
2. The plant is quickly immersed in liquid nitrogen __very slowly__. 
3. Remove plant carefully from liquid nitrogen (Aphids cannot be brushed away since plant is too fragile).  
4. Liquid nitrogen is poured in the mortar and plant is placed in the mortar.  
5. The frozen plant is placed and broken into small fragments in the large funnel.
6. Clean funnel between each sample. 
7. Transfer plant fragments into the falcon tubes (1 plant per falcon).     
8. Place the falcon in liquid nitrogen in the Dewar jar.   

### Sample lyophilization and grinding

10. Transfer tubes to a cryobox.  
11. Place box inside the freeze-dryer at -40°C.   
12. All samples are freeze-dried for 9 days at -40°C then -30°C. then -20°C. A maximum number of [FIXME] of 50ml Falcon tubes can be done at once. 
13. Grind the dried plant material into a fine homogenized powder by vigorously shaking for 4 min using a paint shaker [FIXME: ref].  
14. Weight each Falcon tube + beads + dry sample powder = sample dry weight.
