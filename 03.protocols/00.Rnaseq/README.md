# RNAseq protocol

## Material:  
liquide Nitrogen  
ladle  
Scissors  
Brush  
Aluminium foil  
Falcon 50ml  
Bac polystyrène stockage carré  
rectangle and flat polystyrene box (manip)  
Wooden toothpick  
Marker  
stainless steel beads (3 mm)  

## Equipement:  
Paint shaker  
freeze-dryer  


## Method:  
After 2 weeks of growth, above-ground parts of the plant seedlings are harvested and rapidly frozen in liquid nitrogen  
Put the aluminium foil in the manipulation box  
Pour some nitrogen on the aluminium  
Plant goes into the polyester box on the aluminium foil  
Plant will be brushed to remove aphids  
Aluminium foil is foiled with the plant inside  
Holes are punched into the aluminium with the toothpick  
Aluminium foil goes in the storing polystyrene  
Once every 5 sample more nitrogen is poured in the storage box  
The box is closed  
The storage box stays in the freezer overnight  
The storage box with all the samples is freeze-dried for 24 h   
The plant extract goes from the aluminium to the falcon 50 tubes  
Dried plant material are homogenized into a fine powder by adding three stainless steel beads (3 mm Ø) in each tube and vigorously shaking for four min in a paint shaker
