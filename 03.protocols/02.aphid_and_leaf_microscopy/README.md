# Leaf and aphid fixation`

## Goal

To perform microscopic observation of aphid stylet inside _Vicia faba_ leaf tissues in order to:
- Quantify callose deposition in several contrasted _Vicia faba_ genotypes (contrasted regarding aphid fecundity).  
- Include leaf tissues in order to perform laser microdissection followed by RNA-seq.  

## Protocol from Kristina Schaëdel (MPI-Chemical Ecology, Jena, Germany)

1. Cut off the leaves with the adult, still feeding aphids.   
2. Place the cut leaves in a petri dish.   
3. Place the petri dish on our CO2 table and cover it with a lid.   
4. Incubate everything with CO2 for approx. 20 seconds.    
5. Pour tert. Butanol/PFA solution/fixative (Σ 50ml - 15ml PFA + 35ml tert.butanol) into this petri dish - be careful not to wash the aphids off the leaf.  
6. Incubate everything for a few minutes.
7. Cut the leaf around the preferred aphid and always stay in the fixative.  
8. Then remove the fixative with a pipette and pour some agarose (1%) on the separated aphids.  

The next steps, such as embedding, can follow.
